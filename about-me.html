Design Process

R

Research

Research topic, audience, and map any constraints or requests.

C

Conceptualize

Explore visual inspiration and save possible design references.

W

Wireframe

Sketch the layout/structure, and begin notes on what works and what falls short.

P

Prototype

Mockup the prototype to have both design and functionality then begin bug testing.

F

Refine

Refine the final product, user experience and functionality should be seamless by this stage.
