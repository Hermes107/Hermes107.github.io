MyAbility

"Our team focuses on three primary activity areas that are critical to supporting students with mobility disabilities: access to disability-related information, access to resources and community connections, and access to recommendations for appropriate medical providers. The three areas together shape the students’ ability to manage their health, build trusted, safe support systems, and participate fully in academic and social environments."

Problem Statement & Personas

College students with mobility disabilities need a way to find resources,
research, and recommendations for medical providers in their area
because students with disabilities need to find providers near their college.

Wireframe

The affinity diagram was built using user interviews, this diagram then served as the basis for building 6 task flows 2 of which are featured here. After receiving more data via survey responses updates were made to persona's and the journey map. Addtionally featured here are the components built in Figma, these components were designed by Zoe and each one provides a function for the working prototype and multiple versions of the same elements to fit each different section of the app.

Design Process

The My Ability app is a student-centered mobile platform designed to support college students with mobility disabilities by integrating discoveries for healthcare providers, on/off campus and community resources, and disability related research into a single, unified, accessible experience.

Future Plans

The long term objective of this app would include students with a variety of disabilities both chronic and acute. With the addition of new types of users the app would be developed to have a plethora of new features. Including but not limited to a glossary of terms related to disabilities, healthcare and insurance, a search bar that includes a microphone feature, increased font size to help with card differentiation and accessibility friendly indications on cards, and sections.
