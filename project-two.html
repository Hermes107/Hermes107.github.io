My Story

I have had both PAO and FO surgeries because of hip dysplasia. For I suffered major setbacks because of both surgeries but due to issues with my lateral femoral cutaneous nerve, I struggled the most after my PAO. The goal of this website is to provide. a community and information resource for young adults like me. It should serve as a place where they can learn they are not alone. and connect with each other without the advice or assumptions of older/young people who have vastly different experiences. This website is coming. Not just from a place of research and necessity but also from personal experience as someone who lived through both surgeries and really needed a website like this.
